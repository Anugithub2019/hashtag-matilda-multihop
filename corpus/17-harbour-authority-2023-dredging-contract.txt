Inner-harbour dredging contract awarded
Port Calder Harbour Authority, media statement, 28 June 2023

The Port Calder Harbour Authority has awarded the inner-harbour dredging contract to Greywater Marine. The contract is worth twenty-four million dollars and will deepen the main channel and the north and south wharf berths so that larger cargo ships can use the port.

"Greywater offered the best value and the strongest environmental plan of the four bidders," said the Authority's chief executive, Priya Raman. "Its crews know this harbour better than anyone."

Work will begin in September and is expected to take two years. Silt will be tested and taken to an approved site offshore.
