Kelp & Co chef wins Golden Ladle
The Calder Courier, 24 November 2023

Hana Sato of Kelp & Co has won the 2023 Golden Ladle, the Calder Coast Food Awards' top prize for a chef.

Sato opened Kelp & Co on the Port Calder waterfront three years ago, cooking a small menu built around seaweed and shellfish from local farms. The panel praised her "restraint and nerve".

Sato said she would use the prize money to start a paid apprenticeship for two young cooks next year.
