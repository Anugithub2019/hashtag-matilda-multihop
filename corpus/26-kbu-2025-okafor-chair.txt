University announces Okafor Chair in Marine Chemistry
Kestrel Bay University news, 20 March 2025

Kestrel Bay University will create the Okafor Chair in Marine Chemistry, a permanent professorship named after the university's founding professor in the field.

The chair is funded by a gift of two and a half million dollars from Halcyon Therapeutics. Vice-Chancellor Professor Elena Marsh said the gift was "a debt repaid to the place where a remarkable idea began".

The university will advertise the position internationally later this year.
