Hollis elected mayor in landslide
Wendmoor Gazette, 24 October 2022

Former restaurateur Beatrice Hollis has been elected Mayor of Wendmoor, winning 61 per cent of first preferences in Saturday's council election.

Hollis, who stood as an independent, campaigned on reopening the town's public pool and attracting new industry to the district. Her best-known previous job was running The Copper Pot for seventeen years. "I've balanced a budget with no margin for error," she told supporters at the bowling club. "Council can't be harder than a Saturday night with two chefs off sick."

The outgoing mayor, Ken Abernathy, did not recontest.
