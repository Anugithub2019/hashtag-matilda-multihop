Brightline co-founder to run Port Calder harbour
Calder Business Weekly, 2 March 2022

Priya Raman will leave Brightline Capital, the investment firm she founded with Desmond Faulk in 2016, to become chief executive of the Port Calder Harbour Authority this month.

Ms Raman spent six years at Brightline, where she led its investments in logistics and infrastructure. Mr Faulk remains managing partner of the firm.

"I've spent years telling companies how to run ports," Ms Raman said. "It's time I ran one."

The Authority's board chair, Graham Petrakis, said Ms Raman's experience in both finance and infrastructure made her the clear choice. She replaces Alan Moody, who retired in December.
