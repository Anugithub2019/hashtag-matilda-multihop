Transcript excerpt: "Back of House" podcast, episode 112, March 2025
Guest: Mateo Ruiz

HOST: Before Saltgrass, before the Golden Ladle, where did you learn?

RUIZ: Wendmoor. I was sous chef at The Copper Pot for five years, from 2016. People on the coast forget there's food inland. Beatrice ran that place like a ship. She'd taste every sauce before service. If it wasn't right, it went in the bin, no discussion. She's running the whole town now, which doesn't surprise me at all.

HOST: And the Copper Pot closed?

RUIZ: The kitchen burned. The winter of 2020. Nobody was hurt, thank God, but the building was gone. Beatrice decided not to rebuild. I was cooking in my sister's kitchen for a while, and then Tomas rang me about the customs house.

HOST: Do you still go back?

RUIZ: To Wendmoor? Every few months. There's a bakery there I'd fight someone for.
