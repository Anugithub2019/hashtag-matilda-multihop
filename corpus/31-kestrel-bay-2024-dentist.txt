Free dental checks for Kestrel Bay primary students
Kestrel Bay Community Bulletin, July 2024

Every primary school student in Kestrel Bay will be offered a free dental check this term, thanks to a program run by local paediatric dentist Dr Samuel Osei-Mensah.

Dr Osei-Mensah and two hygienists will visit each of the town's four primary schools with a mobile dental van. Parents will receive a short report and a referral if their child needs treatment.

"Most dental problems in children are completely preventable if we catch them early," Dr Osei-Mensah said. He has practised in Kestrel Bay since 2015.
