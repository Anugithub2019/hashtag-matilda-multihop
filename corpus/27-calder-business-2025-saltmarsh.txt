Biotech co-founder turns to water sensors
Calder Business Weekly, 3 February 2025

Rafael Duarte, who co-founded the antibiotic developer now called Halcyon Therapeutics, has started a new company that makes low-cost sensors for checking water quality in oyster farms and estuaries.

Saltmarsh Analytics will be based in Kestrel Bay and has six staff. "Halcyon is in good hands and it's a clinical company now," Mr Duarte said. "I'm an engineer. I wanted to build things again."

The company is funded by Mr Duarte and two oyster growers. It plans to sell its first sensors in spring.
