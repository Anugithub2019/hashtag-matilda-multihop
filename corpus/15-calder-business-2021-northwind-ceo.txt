Northwind names new chief executive
Calder Business Weekly, 6 September 2021

Northwind Renewables has appointed Callum Reyes as chief executive, effective immediately.

Mr Reyes joins from Greywater Marine, the Port Calder dredging and heavy-lift company, where he spent six years as chief financial officer. Before that he worked in project finance in the city.

Northwind chair Deborah Imrie said the board wanted someone who understood large physical projects as well as money. "Building wind farms is a logistics business," she said. "Callum has spent years moving heavy things across water on time and on budget."

Northwind is planning wind farms in three districts on the Calder Coast.
