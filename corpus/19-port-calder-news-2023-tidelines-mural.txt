"Tidelines" brings colour to the ferry terminal
Port Calder News, 21 November 2023

Commuters at the Port Calder ferry terminal now walk past forty metres of painted sea. "Tidelines", a mural by the artist Odile Fenwick, was unveiled on Monday along the terminal's long eastern wall.

The Harbour Authority commissioned the mural in February. Its chief executive, Priya Raman, said she wanted "the first thing people see when they arrive by water to be about the water". The mural follows a single wave from the open ocean to the reef shelf at Kestrel Bay, with seabirds, kelp and a small red alga hidden throughout.

Fenwick spent seven months on scaffolding to paint it. "People keep asking about the red weed," she said. "That's a private joke with my family."
