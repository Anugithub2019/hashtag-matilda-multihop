Trucking depot opens on highway
Wendmoor Gazette, 11 March 2024

Brightline Logistics has opened a forty-truck depot on the highway east of Wendmoor, its first base outside the city.

Regional manager Steve Kowalczyk said the depot would serve grain growers and the new construction projects in the district. The company employs twenty-six drivers and mechanics at the site.

Brightline Logistics is owned by the Haddad family and has been trucking freight across the state since 1988.
