Studio visit: Odile Fenwick
Calder Arts Notebook, February 2024

Odile Fenwick paints in a tin shed behind her house in Kestrel Bay, with the door open to the wind. She grew up in Wendmoor, the younger of two daughters of a schoolteacher and a stock agent, and says she did not see the sea properly until she was twelve.

Since her ferry terminal mural was unveiled last year, she has been flooded with requests. She turns most of them down. "I paint what I know," she says. "And what I know is mostly water and family."

Family runs through her work. Her older sister, Imogen, is a marine scientist who took her husband's surname, Varga, when she married, and Fenwick says many of her sea paintings began as conversations with her. "She tells me what's living in a rock pool, and I try to paint how it feels."

Her next show opens at the Kestrel Bay regional gallery in June.
