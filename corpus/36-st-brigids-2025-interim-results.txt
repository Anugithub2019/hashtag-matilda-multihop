Encouraging early results for marivex
St Brigid's Hospital, Port Calder, staff newsletter, August 2025

An early look at results from the marivex study run at St Brigid's suggests the experimental antibiotic works at least as well as the current standard treatment for complicated skin infections, with fewer patients stopping treatment because of side effects.

An independent committee reviewed results from the first 80 patients and recommended that the study continue to completion without changes.

Dr Samuel Osei, who leads the study, thanked the patients who took part. "These are early numbers," he said. "But after a difficult start this year, we are pleased."

Full results are expected in 2026.
