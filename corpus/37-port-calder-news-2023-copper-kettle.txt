Copper Kettle celebrates ten years on Wharf Street
Port Calder News, 3 August 2023

The Copper Kettle, the café on Wharf Street known for its breakfasts and its queue of dock workers at 6 am, turns ten this month.

Owner and chef Priyanka Desai opened the café in 2013 with two tables and a borrowed coffee machine. It now seats sixty. She has never worked in another town's restaurant, she says. "I learned in my mother's kitchen and then here."

The café will serve free coffee on its birthday, 15 August.
