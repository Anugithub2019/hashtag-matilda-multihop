Saltgrass chef takes the Golden Ladle
The Calder Courier, 22 November 2024

Mateo Ruiz, head chef at Saltgrass in Kestrel Bay, has won the 2024 Golden Ladle, the Calder Coast Food Awards' top prize for a chef.

The judging panel was chaired by the Courier's chief restaurant critic, Winifred Asante, and visited thirty-four restaurants between June and October. "It was not close," Ms Asante said. "He cooks the coast better than anyone working on it."

The other finalists were Priyanka Desai of the Copper Kettle in Port Calder and Liam Brannagh of Fernhill in Mirrin Falls.

Ruiz thanked his team and the restaurant's owner, and dedicated the award to "the people who taught me in a small kitchen that isn't there any more".

The awards dinner was held at the Port Calder ferry terminal for the first time.
