Giant blades come ashore at Port Calder
Port Calder News, 10 October 2024

The first shipment of turbine blades for the Ridgeback project was lifted onto the north wharf this week, each blade longer than the ferry that crosses the harbour.

The blades were built by the German manufacturer Altmark Turbinen and spent six weeks at sea. Greywater Marine handled the heavy lift from the ship and will move the blades onto specialist trailers for the night-time drive inland to the construction site.

Eighty-four blades will arrive in four shipments before March. Roads between the port and the ridge will be closed between midnight and 5 am on delivery nights.
