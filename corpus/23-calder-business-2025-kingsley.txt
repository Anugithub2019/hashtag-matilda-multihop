New chief for Port Calder Harbour Authority
Calder Business Weekly, 5 May 2025

Gareth Kingsley has started as chief executive of the Port Calder Harbour Authority, succeeding Priya Raman, who stepped down at the end of April to return to investing.

Mr Kingsley was previously operations director at a container terminal interstate. His first priority, he said, would be restoring the north wharf to full use after the damage it suffered in February.

Ms Raman led the Authority for a little over three years. During that time it signed the inner-harbour dredging contract and commissioned new public art for the ferry terminal.
