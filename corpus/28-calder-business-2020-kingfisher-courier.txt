Kingfisher Media buys The Calder Courier
Calder Business Weekly, 30 October 2020

The Calder Courier, the region's oldest newspaper, has been sold to Kingfisher Media, which owns two regional radio stations and a printing plant.

Kingfisher's chairman, Desmond Faulk, said the company would keep the newspaper's printed edition for at least five years and would hire four more reporters. "A region without a newspaper stops knowing itself," he said.

Mr Faulk is also managing partner of the investment firm Brightline Capital. He said Kingfisher was a separate business and that he would have no role in the Courier's newsroom.
