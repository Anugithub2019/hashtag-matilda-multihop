First patients join seaweed antibiotic study at St Brigid's
St Brigid's Hospital, Port Calder, staff newsletter, September 2024

St Brigid's has enrolled the first patients in a Phase II study of marivex, an experimental antibiotic developed by Halcyon Therapeutics.

The study will recruit 120 adults with complicated skin and soft-tissue infections over about a year. Half will receive marivex and half will receive the current standard antibiotic. Neither the patients nor the treating doctors will know which treatment a patient is given.

The study is led by Dr Samuel Osei, an infectious-diseases physician who has worked at St Brigid's for eleven years. "Every week I see an infection that our usual drugs no longer touch," Dr Osei said. "If marivex works, those patients get another option."

Staff who would like to refer a patient should contact the infectious-diseases unit on level four.
