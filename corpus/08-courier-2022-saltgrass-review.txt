Review: Saltgrass, Kestrel Bay
The Calder Courier, 9 April 2022, by Winifred Asante

Saltgrass opened last month in the old customs house on the Kestrel Bay foreshore, and it is already hard to get a table on a Friday.

The owner, Tomas Lindqvist, spent years working on dredges and tugs before deciding he would rather feed sailors than be one. He greets nearly every table himself. The kitchen belongs to head chef Mateo Ruiz, who has come to the coast from inland and cooks as if he has been waiting years for decent fish.

Order the flathead with burnt butter and sea herbs. Order the smoked mussels. Do not skip the bread, which arrives with a cultured butter made a few streets away. The wine list is short and local and fairly priced.

Service was warm but slow on a full night, and the dining room gets loud. Neither will matter to anyone who has eaten the flathead.

Four stars.
