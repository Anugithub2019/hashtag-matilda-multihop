Tidewell Seafoods to double processing plant
Mirrin Falls Recorder, 8 May 2024

Tidewell Seafoods will double the size of its fish processing plant on the Mirrin Falls river flats, adding sixty jobs over the next two years.

Chief executive Imogen Price said demand for frozen whiting and flathead from supermarkets in the city had grown faster than the company could keep up with. "We are turning orders away," she said. "That is a good problem, but it's still a problem."

The family-owned company was founded by Ms Price's grandfather in 1961. It buys fish from about forty independent boats along the Calder Coast.

Ms Price said the company was regularly contacted by people looking for "the medicine company with our name". "We get their mail, their job applications and once a very confused courier with a box of laboratory glass," she said.
