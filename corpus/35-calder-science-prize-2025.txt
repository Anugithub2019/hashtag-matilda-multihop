2025 Calder Science Prize goes to seagrass researcher
Calder Science Foundation, announcement, 8 September 2025

The 2025 Calder Science Prize has been awarded to Dr Lena Kovac of Kestrel Bay University for her work measuring how much carbon seagrass meadows on the Calder Coast store.

The jury was again chaired by Emeritus Professor Harold Okafor, who said Dr Kovac's work had "changed how the state counts its coastline".

Last year's prize went to the developer of a new antibiotic.
