Update on our Phase II study
Halcyon Therapeutics, investor letter, 14 April 2025

Dear shareholders,

Recruitment into our Phase II study of marivex is now running about six weeks behind plan. The cause was not the study itself. February's storm damaged the north wharf at Port Calder, the only berth in the region that can handle our temperature-controlled supply shipments, and for several weeks the study site could not receive new drug supplies. Enrolment of new patients had to stop until shipments resumed.

Shipments resumed in late March after emergency repairs, and enrolment has restarted. We now expect to complete recruitment in the third quarter. The delay does not change our cash runway.

We also want to thank Rafael Duarte, who left us in January after nearly eight years. Rafael built our first laboratory with his own hands, and we wish him well.

Dr Imogen Varga
Chief Executive
