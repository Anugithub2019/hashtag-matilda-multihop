Brightline writes biggest cheque yet for seaweed drug company
Calder Business Weekly, 12 March 2024

Tidewell Bio has raised thirty-eight million dollars in a round led by Brightline Capital, money the company says will carry its lead antibiotic, marivex, through a mid-stage human trial.

Brightline's managing partner, Desmond Faulk, will join the company's board. "Most antibiotic start-ups die between the laboratory and the clinic because nobody will fund that gap," Mr Faulk said. "We think this one deserves to get across it." It is the largest single investment Brightline has made since it was formed.

Sandpiper Ventures, which led the company's earlier round, also invested.

Chief executive Dr Imogen Varga said the company would begin recruiting patients for a Phase II study later this year and would need a larger home. She declined to say where the company would move, saying only that an announcement would come "within months, along with a few other changes".
