Fire destroys Wendmoor restaurant
The Calder Courier, 14 July 2021

A fire that started in the kitchen of The Copper Pot on Wendmoor's main street late on Monday night has destroyed the restaurant. Staff had gone home and no one was injured. Fire crews stopped the blaze from spreading to the newsagency next door.

Owner Beatrice Hollis, who opened The Copper Pot in 2004, said on Tuesday that she would not rebuild. "Seventeen years is a good run," she said, standing across the street from the blackened shopfront. "I'll find another way to be useful to this town."

Investigators believe a fault in an extraction fan caused the fire. Ms Hollis said all eleven staff would be paid for the rest of the month.
