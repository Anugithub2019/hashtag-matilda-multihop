Swim coach Okafor honoured for 30 years at the pool
Mirrin Falls Recorder, 19 December 2024

Harry Okafor, who has coached the Mirrin Falls High School swim team for thirty years, has been named the town's Citizen of the Year.

Mr Okafor has trained three state champions and taught several thousand local children to swim at the town pool. "He was at the pool at six every morning for three decades," said principal Joanne Feld.

Mr Okafor said he had no plans to retire. "My knees disagree," he said, "but they don't get a vote."
