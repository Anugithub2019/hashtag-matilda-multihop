Cyclone Maren batters Port Calder
Port Calder News, 12 February 2025

Cyclone Maren crossed the coast south of Port Calder early on Tuesday, bringing winds of up to 150 kilometres an hour and a storm surge that tore into the harbour.

The north wharf took the worst of it. A section of the deck collapsed, two cranes were damaged and the wharf has been closed to shipping until further notice. A Harbour Authority spokesperson said cargo would be redirected to the south wharf where possible, but refrigerated and oversized cargo could not be handled there.

Greywater Marine, which was already working in the harbour on the dredging project, has been contracted to clear debris and carry out emergency repairs to the north wharf.

More than 9,000 homes across the region lost power. No deaths have been reported.
