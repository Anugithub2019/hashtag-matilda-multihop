Council narrowly backs Ridgeback wind farm
Wendmoor Gazette, 17 August 2023

Wendmoor council has approved the Ridgeback Wind project, a 28-turbine wind farm on the ridgeline west of town, by five votes to four after a meeting that ran past midnight.

The vote was tied at four-all among councillors until the mayor, Beatrice Hollis, used her vote to approve the plan. "This district has watched its young people leave for forty years," she said. "Two hundred construction jobs and a lease income for twelve farming families is not something I am willing to turn away."

Opponents argued the turbines would spoil views from the Wendmoor lookout and threaten wedge-tailed eagles.

The developer, Northwind Renewables, expects construction to begin next year. Its chief executive, Callum Reyes, attended the meeting and thanked the council for "a hard, fair hearing".
