Algae antibiotic developer raises six million dollars
Calder Business Weekly, 22 August 2019

Tidewell Bio, the Kestrel Bay company working on antibiotics from seaweed, has closed a six million dollar funding round led by Sandpiper Ventures.

The money will pay for animal studies of the company's lead compound, which the team has named marivex. Marivex was isolated from a red alga collected off the Kestrel Bay headland, and in laboratory tests it was active against bacteria that cause hard-to-treat skin and wound infections.

Moira Delaney, the founder of Sandpiper Ventures, will take a seat on the company's board. "We back scientists who have done the slow part already," she said. "This team spent years in the water before they spent a dollar of anyone else's money."

Chief executive Dr Imogen Varga said the company would hire six scientists and move out of its boatshed into a leased laboratory near the university by the end of the year. Co-founder Rafael Duarte remains chief technology officer.

The round is one of the largest early-stage investments in a Calder Coast life-science company this decade.
