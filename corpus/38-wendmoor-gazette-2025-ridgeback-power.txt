First power from Ridgeback
Wendmoor Gazette, 30 June 2025

The first turbines at Ridgeback Wind began sending power to the grid this week. All 28 turbines are expected to be running by the end of the year.

Construction was slowed in February when the last shipment of blades could not be unloaded at Port Calder after the cyclone, and the ship was sent north to Kestrel Bay instead, where the blades were unloaded by barge. The detour added about three weeks.

Mayor Beatrice Hollis said the first lease payments had already reached farming families on the ridge. "People said this town couldn't attract anything new," she said. "Look up."
