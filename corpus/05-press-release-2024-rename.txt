Tidewell Bio is now Halcyon Therapeutics
Press release, 3 June 2024

Tidewell Bio today announced that it has changed its name to Halcyon Therapeutics and will move its headquarters from Kestrel Bay to the Port Calder innovation precinct in August.

"Our old name served us well in the boatshed years," said chief executive Dr Imogen Varga. "But we were regularly mistaken for an unrelated seafood business, and as we enter clinical trials we need a name that belongs only to us."

The company's research laboratory will stay in Kestrel Bay. All existing partnerships, sponsorships and agreements continue under the new name. The company's antibiotic candidate, marivex, keeps its name.

Halcyon Therapeutics was founded in 2017 and employs forty-one people.
