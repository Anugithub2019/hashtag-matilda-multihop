2024 Calder Science Prize awarded for seaweed antibiotic
Calder Science Foundation, announcement, 9 September 2024

The 2024 Calder Science Prize has been awarded to Dr Imogen Varga for the discovery and development of marivex, an antibiotic derived from a red alga native to the Calder Coast.

The jury was chaired by Emeritus Professor Harold Okafor. "This is the kind of science the prize was created for," he said. "Patient, local and with consequences far beyond the coast."

The prize carries fifty thousand dollars. Dr Varga said she would donate it to a scholarship for marine science students from inland towns.
