New mural for the university library
Kestrel Bay University news, 4 April 2024

A new mural by the artist Bruno Achterberg now covers the northern wall of the Kestrel Bay University library. The university commissioned the work to mark the library's fiftieth anniversary.

The mural, titled "Shelf", shows the reef shelf off the Kestrel Bay headland as a set of library shelves, with fish, sponges and seaweed arranged like books.

Achterberg, who lives in Mirrin Falls, painted the mural over the summer break.
