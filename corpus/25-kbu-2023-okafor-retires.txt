Professor Harold Okafor retires after 25 years
Kestrel Bay University news, 15 December 2023

Professor Harold Okafor, who joined Kestrel Bay University in 1998 as its first professor of marine chemistry, retires this month.

Over his career he supervised thirty-two doctoral students. Many now work in industry and government, and at least one has built a company from her doctoral work. Imogen Varga completed her doctorate in his laboratory in 2013, with a thesis on defensive chemicals in red algae on the Kestrel Bay reef shelf.

"Harold taught me that if you want to understand the sea, you have to get wet," Dr Varga said at his farewell.

Professor Okafor, who was born in 1951, says he plans to fish, read and "finally finish the garden". He has been appointed Emeritus Professor.
