Boat owners face higher premiums after cyclone
Port Calder News, 2 June 2025

Insurance premiums for commercial and private boats in Port Calder are set to rise by up to thirty per cent after Cyclone Maren.

Greywater Maritime Insurance, a Port Calder broker that specialises in marine policies, said insurers were still assessing claims from February. "Every insurer is repricing the coast," said its managing director, Fiona Leckie. Greywater Maritime Insurance has no connection to the dredging company of a similar name, she added.

The broker advised owners to check whether their policies covered storm surge, which many older policies did not.
