Mariners unveil new shirt partner for the next three seasons
Port Calder Mariners Football Club, club news, 16 January 2023

The Port Calder Mariners are proud to announce Tidewell Bio as the club's principal shirt partner for the 2023, 2024 and 2025 seasons. The partner's logo will appear on the front of the home and away shirts, and the club's academy will run joint school visits with the company's scientists.

Club captain Jasper Quill said the squad had visited the partner's laboratory in Kestrel Bay before Christmas. "I didn't understand half of it, but they're local people doing something that could matter to everyone," he said. "That's what this club is about."

The partnership also funds a new physiotherapy room at the Harbourside Oval training ground.

Members can collect the new shirts from the club shop from 1 February. Season tickets remain on sale until the opening round against Mirrin Falls.
